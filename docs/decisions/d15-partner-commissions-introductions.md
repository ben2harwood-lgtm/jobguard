## Exact proposed policy

JobGuard may receive third-party money only from insurers (for example insurance-backed guarantees registered from the evidence pack, as an introducer or under an appointed-representative arrangement) and from professional bodies' member schemes, always disclosed to the builder. JobGuard may pay referral shares to trade bodies and accountants, disclosed in the builder's terms. Never from merchants, suppliers, lenders, lead platforms or the builder's customers. Only builder-consented, builder-visible facts reach a partner; no partner sees Final Check signals.
