provider effect IDs, signed events, allocations, statement/invoice revisions,
posted journals, provider clearing, refunds and reversals. Checkout redirects and
pending receipts are not settled money. Confirm one business effect per source
and balanced books. Preserve `outcome_unknown`, reconcile without resending, and
use the rollback plan on any discrepancy. Do not delete or rewrite postings.

all discrepancies, their resolution and evidence against the exact commit and
configuration. An independent reviewer must review the reconciled smoke before
general rollout. Missing reconciliation, unresolved uncertainty or changed scope
