# Pilot terms contract v1

`pilot_no_charge_v1` permits the M1 real-data loop only after G1 and only within approved D02/D06 scope. It creates no JobGuard plan or recovery-fee obligation, collection request, or production journal. Fee examples are non-posting illustrations under `reference_fee_policy_v1`. Builder/customer money records remain distinct from platform accounting. No pilot activity may be billed retrospectively without a separately approved agreement and migration.
