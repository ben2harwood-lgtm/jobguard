# Homeowner pilot privacy notice

**Unapproved draft for Ben and the solicitor (PILOT-15/16). Do not publish or distribute.**

Your builder may use JobGuard to prepare quotes, keep a record of work and agreed changes, and provide job documents. The job record may include your contact details, the work address, messages, quote and acceptance records, and evidence such as site photographs. Your builder may provide this information to JobGuard. JobGuard may also receive information directly from you when you use the customer portal. Portal messages become job records and may be reviewed as evidence when the builder checks the final account. The pilot may analyse job records to flag possible omissions for the builder to review.

Access to job records is limited to authorised parties. Approved service providers may process information to operate and secure the service. Some records may need to be retained for legal or accounting purposes. Restricted backup copies expire on their approved schedule.

You can ask for a copy or correction of your personal information and, where applicable, ask for erasure, restriction or portability or object to processing. We verify your identity and any representative's authority before releasing information. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
