# Builder pilot privacy notice

**Unapproved draft for Ben and the solicitor (PILOT-15/16). Do not publish or distribute.**

JobGuard would use your account details, job records and service activity to run and secure the pilot. Job records can include scope notes, quote versions and acceptance, diary entries, photos, supporting documents, variations, customer messages and payment records. Account, job and security records have different purposes and retention needs.

JobGuard analyses job records for possible unrecorded extras. If the Final Check is available for your job, JobGuard compares the records independently with the final account you complete and presents possible omissions for your review. Findings are proposals for you to review. The pilot creates no JobGuard subscription or recovery fee. A later, separately agreed service may charge a success fee on paid recoveries that qualify.

Enter only information needed for the job. When a record contains someone else's information, follow the agreed procedure for giving them the homeowner notice. JobGuard would share job data with approved service providers where necessary to operate the pilot and with authorised recipients when you direct a commercial send.

You can ask to access or correct your personal information or ask us to erase or restrict it. Other rights, including objection and portability, apply in some circumstances. We verify requests and protect other people's information when responding. Some records may need to be kept for legal or accounting purposes. Copies in restricted backups expire on their approved schedule. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

**Resolve before approval.** Insert the controller's legal identity and privacy contact, each purpose and lawful basis, any legitimate interests, recipients, locations and transfer safeguards, retention periods or criteria, and the consequences of declining required information. Confirm the applicable controller roles and the timing for giving this notice before the first job.
