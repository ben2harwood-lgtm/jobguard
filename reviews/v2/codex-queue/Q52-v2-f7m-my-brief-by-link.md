## Planner reading (Astra please confirm or correct)
1. **One progress stream.** A crew member's Done, Blocked or Changed is a row in F1's `app.v2_brief_progress`, on the same per-task version chain as the builder's own presses. So F2's `taskProofStatus` (`packages/db/src/v2-proof-status.ts`) and the builder's Brief progress screen see crew presses with no extra code. The row records **who** pressed: a builder membership **or** a crew link, never both.
2. **No photos from the link in this piece.** The mock says "The camera comes later". A crew "Done" on a task that needs proof shows as `done_proof_missing` until the builder adds the photo through F1-P. Crew photo by link is a later piece (F7M-P).
3. **The link shows the latest generated brief for its trade, never a rebuilt one.** Freshness is checked in SQL by a conservative rule (step 1e). If the plan moved since the brief was generated, the page says "Your brief has changed" and records nothing, which is the mock's "Changed" banner, until the builder regenerates.
4. **No sending.** The builder copies the link from the screen. The 6pm crew list is E8. Share to WhatsApp is K1's pattern, later.
5. **Copies E2's token pattern; does not stack on E2.** E2 #949 is on another stack. This piece copies its shape (hash only, per-transaction scope row, definer open/receive/close, coarse failure limit, audit through a narrow definer) under its own names.

## Tests
- **Seed tests** (appended, repository on the `runtime` pool with a fixed clock):
  1. **Done-when case.** The tiler on the practice job gets a link; opening it shows only tiling tasks, no price; pressing Done on a task with no proof needed makes F2's status `done` on the builder's screen; Done on a task that needs proof makes it `done_proof_missing`; Blocked with a reason and Changed with a note show on the builder's screen "by link".
  2. **Confinement:** the page JSON holds no price, value, customer name, other trade's task, other job, phone, email or token; a decorator's link can't press a tiling task; a link for job A can't name job B's task.
  3. Refusals, all `CREW_LINK_REFUSED` with the same body: made-up token, expired, cancelled, replaced by a newer link, crew record corrected since issue; a refused press writes nothing.
  4. `briefChanged`: a plan edit after the brief was generated makes the page say so and refuses a press; regenerating the brief lets the next press through.
  5. Version chain: a builder press and a link press on the same task at the same expected version, one wins and the other is a version conflict; replay of the same command returns the stored row; same id with a different body is refused.
  6. Database: a progress row with both or neither actor columns is refused; a link row with a photo is refused; UPDATE and DELETE are refused; a link for a crew record of another firm or another trade is refused.
  7. **Rule 30, firm A/B read check:** the second firm sees no `v2_crew_link` or `v2_crew_link_cancel` rows of the first firm's job, as `jobguard_runtime`.

## Known limits (to write in the PR)
- `briefChanged` is conservative: an edit to another trade's task also marks this trade's brief changed until the builder regenerates.
- A link is a bearer link: anyone the crew member forwards it to can press. The expiry, cancel and newest-link rules bound it. A per-device bind is later.

- An expired, cancelled or replaced link, or a changed plan, records nothing.
