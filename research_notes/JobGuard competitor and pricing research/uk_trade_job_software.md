# UK trade job-management, quoting and invoicing software for small builders (pricing as of Sept 2026)

Research date: 24 September 2026. Vendor pricing pages were fetched directly where possible. Several vendors (Tradify, Fergus, Jobber) served USD pages to this fetcher because of geo-detection. For those, UK GBP figures come from UK review sites that record the date they checked, and are marked as such. "ex VAT" means the source states prices exclude VAT.

## 1. Price points, pricing units, tiers, trials, annual discounts and payment fees

### Takeaway
The UK small-trades market sits in a narrow band of about £25–£45 per user (or per plan) per month, ex VAT. The pricing units are per user (Tradify, Powered Now, Fieldmotion), per plan with add-on users (YourTradebase, Fergus) or per job volume with unlimited users (ServiceM8). Trials are 14 days, or 30 days for YourTradebase, with no card needed. Annual billing usually gives "2 months free". Payment processing is passed through Stripe or the vendor's own processor at card rates. Only Payaca (cleantech, £299–£3,199/mo) and the enterprise field-service tools (Fieldmotion, Joblogic, Simpro, BigChange, Commusoft) sit well above this band.

### Inferences

- ServiceM8's per-job pricing (£25 for 50 jobs) is structurally different and cheap for small builders, who run few, long jobs.
- Checkatrade bundling a free quote/invoice tool with its membership makes "free quoting" a commodity for many UK trades. JobGuard's free-quote positioning is not unique on price alone.
- Annual discounts converge on "2 months free" (about 17%).

## 2. Feature overlap with JobGuard

### Takeaway
Every mainstream tool covers quote → job → invoice → online card payment, and most now offer automatic quote and invoice reminders. The overlap is thinner in the areas that matter most to JobGuard:
- **Variations and change orders:** rarely a first-class feature. Explicitly gated only in Joist (Elite and above) and implied by Fergus job phases.
- **Supplier bill capture:** exists as AI OCR or import in Tradify Plus, ServiceM8 Starter and above, and Powered Now Premium. None was found that checks supplier invoices for overcharges.
- **Post-bill "missed extras" check and recovery of withheld payments:** no product found offers either.
- **Voice and AI:** mostly AI receptionists and writing aids, not voice-to-scope capture.

- **Tradify:**
  - SmartRead for Bills (AI cost capture from supplier bills), SmartWrite (AI drafting) and purchase orders are Plus-only (£44/user).
  - Progress invoicing is Pro and above.

  - Quote and invoice reminders are Pro and above.

### Inferences
- AI supplier-bill capture is a premium-tier differentiator at about £40–£44/user and above (Tradify Plus, Powered Now Premium). This suggests incumbents treat bill capture as an upsell lever. Overcharge detection against quoted or PO prices was not found anywhere.
- None of the reviewed products markets a "final account" (baseline + approved variations → final bill) in the UK builder sense. They market progress or partial invoicing. JobGuard's variation-to-final-account spine and post-lock missed-extras check appear to be unoccupied positioning among small-trade tools.
- Voice capture on incumbents is mostly inbound call answering (PN Receptionist, Jobber, Joist AI receptionists, ServiceM8 Phone transcription), not site-walk scope capture. Fergus Go's "speech" job updates are the nearest overlap.

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether Tradify, ServiceM8 or Fergus have explicit "variation" objects. Feature pages were not fetched in full.
- The official Tradify page and a review disagree on whether job costing is on all tiers.
- No evidence was found of supplier-invoice-vs-quote price matching in any product. This is absence of evidence from pricing and feature pages, not proof that it does not exist.

## 3. Positioning and target customer

### Takeaway
Most tools are field-service or domestic-trades products, built for short, repeatable jobs in plumbing, electrical, gas, HVAC and cleaning, rather than project-based building work with variations. UK reviewers position:
- Tradify: 2–10 person trade firms.
- Powered Now: certificate-heavy regulated trades.
- YourTradebase: cheap, simple sole-trader quoting.
- ServiceM8: staff-heavy, Apple-first crews.
- Payaca: cleantech installers.
- Fieldmotion, Joblogic, Simpro, BigChange and Commusoft: larger firms on long contracts.

### Cited Findings
- Tradify is best for "small trade firms (2–10 people)" wanting straightforward quoting, scheduling and invoicing. — [search summary citing The Builder's Verdict / VioTrade / Brcks](https://thebuildersverdict.co.uk/best/job-management-software)
- Powered Now suits "sole traders in regulated trades (gas, electrical, plumbing)" that need certificates, and VAT-registered trades with an MTD priority. — [The Builder's Verdict](https://thebuildersverdict.co.uk/best/job-management-software); [TradeStash comparison](https://tradestashapp.com/blog/tradify-vs-powered-now-vs-yourtradebase) (via search summary)
- YourTradebase is best for sole traders "who just want to send professional quotes and invoices as cheaply as possible". — [TradeStash comparison](https://tradestashapp.com/blog/tradify-vs-powered-now-vs-yourtradebase) (via search summary)
- ServiceM8 is best for "staff-heavy crews; Apple-first setup". — [The Builder's Verdict](https://thebuildersverdict.co.uk/best/job-management-software)
- Payaca targets clean-tech installers (solar PV, heat pumps, batteries, EV chargers). — [payaca.com/pricing](https://www.payaca.com/pricing)
- Fieldmotion targets FM, HVAC, pest control, fire and security, and renewables. — [fieldmotion.com/pricing](https://fieldmotion.com/pricing/)
- Joblogic targets FM and planned maintenance. Simpro targets 15+ person project-led contractors. BigChange targets fleet-heavy firms with 5+ vehicles. — [DashLink](https://dashlink.media/tradify-alternatives-uk/)
- Jobber targets cleaners, landscapers and handyman businesses, and has no UK gas or electrical certificates. — [DashLink](https://dashlink.media/tradify-alternatives-uk/)
- Checkatrade, MyBuilder and Rated People are lead-generation directories. Only Checkatrade bundles a basic quote and invoice tool. — [Sleepless Tradesman](https://sleeplesstradesman.com/guides/mybuilder-alternatives); [Checkatrade blog](https://www.checkatrade.com/blog/news/checkatrade-quoting-and-invoicing-tool/)

### Inferences
- Small general builders (extensions, refurbs, lofts) are served by trade tools designed around service calls. The strongest fit among incumbents for project-style work is Fergus (job phases, purchase orders), Tradify Plus (purchase orders, bill capture) or Simpro at the high end. This leaves a gap for a builder-specific, variation-centric product priced in the £30–£60/mo band.

### Gaps
- Could not find UK trade press (Professional Builder, BMJ, PHPI) coverage comparing these tools within the call budget.

## 5. Does any product charge success fees or take a % of recovered or collected money?

### Takeaway
No reviewed product charges a success fee or a percentage of recovered or withheld money. The only percentage-of-money charges are ordinary card-processing fees (for example Fergus UK at 1.6% + £0.20, Jobber US at 2.9% + 30¢, and Stripe pass-through for Tradify and ServiceM8). Checkatrade's pay-per-lead is a percentage-like, lead-based charge rather than a recovery fee. A 10% success fee on recovered money would therefore be novel in this category, and buyers would compare it with debt-collection pricing rather than with software pricing.

### Cited Findings
- Fergus UK card processing costs 1.6% + £0.20 per transaction. — [fergus.com/uk/pricing](https://fergus.com/uk/pricing/)
- Jobber card processing costs 2.9% + 30¢ (US figures), with an extra 1% for instant payouts. — [getjobber.com/en-gb/pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/en-gb/pricing/)
- Tradify UK uses Stripe, at "£0.17 + a percentage ... depending on card type". — [Tradify UK Stripe page](https://www.tradifyhq.com/uk/credit-card-payments-with-stripe) (via search summary)
- ServiceM8 fees are Stripe pay-as-you-go per transaction, and the UK prohibits card surcharges in many scenarios. — [ServiceM8 Help](https://support.servicem8.com/hc/en-us/articles/203229660-Stripe-transaction-fees-payout-speeds) (via search summary)
- Checkatrade pay-as-you-go lead fees vary with job value, channel, location and demand. — [LocalAdder / NobleLeads search summaries](https://localadder.co.uk/checkatrade-cost/)
- None of the pricing pages fetched (Tradify, Fergus, ServiceM8, YourTradebase, Powered Now, Jobber, Joist, Payaca, Fieldmotion) mention any success, recovery or collection-percentage fee. — the sources above

### Inferences
- Incumbents monetise with seat subscriptions, tier upsells (AI bill capture, chasing and purchase orders sit in the top tiers), SMS, add-ons such as AI receptionists (£29–£39/mo at Powered Now; $29–$49/mo at Jobber and Joist) and card-processing margins. A subscription at around £30–£45 per user per month plus a success fee would sit at market parity on the subscription. The fee is the novel element that needs justification.

### Gaps
- Pricing of UK trade debt-recovery or collection services (the natural benchmark for a 10% recovery fee) was out of scope for this note and not researched.
