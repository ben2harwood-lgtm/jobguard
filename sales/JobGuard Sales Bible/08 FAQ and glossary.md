# JobGuard Sales Bible — Part 8: FAQ and glossary

This is part 8 of the JobGuard Sales Bible: quick answers to the questions builders ask most, and a glossary of building trade words and JobGuard words.

## Frequently asked questions

### About the product

**What is JobGuard?**
The profit watchdog for builders. You talk through a job once; it becomes the quote, the live job and the final bill. JobGuard watches the job and the money, checks supplier invoices, keeps every extra, and does a Final Check for work you forgot to bill.

**Who is it for?**
UK builders doing project work — extensions, lofts, kitchens, bathrooms, renovations — from sole traders up to firms of about 20 people.

**Does it work on my phone?**
Yes. There's a phone app that works on site even with no signal, and it works on a computer for the office.

**How do I get my job in?**
Talk through it on site like you'd explain it to a mate. JobGuard writes it up as a list of work and shows you what it heard. You check it, fix anything, and it becomes your quote.

**Does the AI decide my prices?**
No. It suggests prices from your own rates. You decide every price. It never sends anything, sets prices or approves anything on its own.

**How do extras work?**
Tap "Log an extra" on the job, say or type what it is, price it, and send it to the customer to approve on their phone. It goes straight onto your final bill.

**Does my customer need an app?**
No. They get a link on their phone or email to accept the quote, approve extras and sign off stages.

**How does it check my supplier invoices?**
Photograph or forward invoices and delivery notes, or connect your merchant account so they come in automatically. JobGuard reads them, matches them against what you ordered and what was delivered, and checks the prices against what you agreed. Anything wrong goes into "Things to check" before you pay.

**What does the Final Check do?**
After you complete your final bill, JobGuard checks the job's receipts, photos, notes and customer messages for work that was done but isn't on the bill. It shows you each item with the proof. You decide whether to bill it.

**Can I bring in jobs I've already started?**
Yes. You can bring in jobs already under way.

**Does it do stage payments, deposits and retention?**
Deposits and stage payments, yes. Retention tracking, Construction Act notices and interim applications are coming later; don't promise them yet.

**Does it do VAT and CIS?**
It puts the right VAT (including reverse charge on business-to-business work) and CIS deductions on your invoices. It doesn't give tax advice; your accountant still does.

**Does it work with Xero or QuickBooks?**
Yes. It sends your figures across. Keep your accounting software for your accountant.

**Can my whole team use it?**
Yes. Unlimited users on every plan: labourers, site managers, the office, whoever does the books. You control what each person can see and do.

### About money and pricing

**How much does it cost?**
Quoting is free. Plans are £29 (Solo, up to 4 jobs on the go) and £69 (Builder, up to 7) a month, plus VAT. Extra jobs over your plan are £20 a month each. Your first live job is free. Firms with more than 7 jobs at once go on the contractor deal instead: 10% of the extras their site staff log, once paid.

**What counts as a job "on the go"?**
A job you're actually working on. It starts counting when work starts on site (or 30 days after you switch it live) and stops when you complete the final account, mark it finished on site, or it's been quiet for a month. Quotes never count. Jobs under £2,000 don't take a place.

**What happens if I go over my number of jobs?**
Nothing gets blocked. Each extra job costs £20 a month, only for the days it's on. When it's cheaper to move up a plan, JobGuard tells you.

**Is there a free trial?**
Your first live job is free until you complete its final account (or for 90 days), as long as it's your only live job. Quoting is always free.

**What's the 10% fee?**
10% of extra money JobGuard finds or recovers for you, and only once you've been paid: work the Final Check found that you then billed and got paid for; a cash refund from a supplier you'd overpaid; or money recovered through a recovery case you chose to open.

**Do you take 10% of my extras?**
Not if you logged them. Anything you log with Log an extra, or add yourself before completing your final bill, is 100% yours.

**Do you take 10% of my normal invoices?**
No. Never on your normal work and never for ordinary reminders or late payments.

**What if my customer doesn't pay?**
Then there's no fee.

**Is the 10% on top of the monthly plan?**
Yes. They're separate. The monthly plan pays for everything JobGuard does every month; the 10% is only on extra money it finds or recovers.

**How do you collect the 10%?**
When your customer pays, you see it on one screen: amount received, JobGuard's fee, what's yours. The fee is collected by Direct Debit shortly after, plus VAT.

**Does JobGuard hold my customers' money?**
No. Your customers pay straight into your account through a regulated payment provider.

**Can I pay yearly?**
Yes: two months free.

### About trust and data

**Where is my data kept?**
In the UK and EU. It's yours and you can export it.

**Can other builders see my jobs or prices?**
No. Each company only sees its own.

**Do you sell my data or my customers' details?**
No. Never.

**Does JobGuard take money from merchants?**
No. JobGuard checks merchants' invoices, so it never takes money from them. That's why you can trust the checks.

**Will you contact my customers?**
Only when you tell it to. Quotes, approvals, invoices and reminders all go out with your approval, in your name.

**Is it a debt collector?**
No. It helps you chase your own money with your own evidence and factual letters that you approve. It doesn't take anyone to court.

**Do you tell me about problems during the job?**
Yes: supplier invoice problems, sites not ready, missing photos, extras awaiting approval. Possible unbilled work is kept for the Final Check after you've done your own bill, unless it's urgent (safety, significant work needing your customer's written OK first, a dispute brewing, or a legal duty), in which case JobGuard tells you straight away and never charges on it.

## Glossary

### Building trade words

- **Variation / extra:** work added to the job after the quote was accepted, usually because the customer asked. JobGuard calls it an "extra".
- **Final account:** the full bill at the end of a job: the original price plus all extras, minus anything taken out.
- **Snagging:** fixing small defects at the end of a job.
- **First fix / second fix:** first fix is the work before walls are closed up (pipes, cables); second fix is after (sockets, taps, fittings).
- **Making good:** repairing and tidying surfaces after the main work.
- **Retention:** a percentage of payments held back by the client until defects are fixed, then released.
- **Stage payment / interim payment:** payments made part-way through a job as stages are completed.
- **Application for payment:** a contractor's formal request for payment for work done so far, common in commercial and subcontract work.
- **Payment notice / pay-less notice:** Construction Act notices for business-to-business contracts. If the payer wants to pay less than applied for, they must send a pay-less notice in time, or the full amount is due.
- **Construction Act:** the UK law setting payment rules and fast dispute resolution (adjudication) for business-to-business construction contracts. It generally doesn't apply to work on a homeowner's own home.
- **Adjudication:** a fast process for deciding payment disputes between businesses under the Construction Act.
- **CIS (Construction Industry Scheme):** contractors deduct tax from payments to subcontractors and pass it to HMRC.
- **VAT reverse charge (DRC):** on many business-to-business construction jobs, the customer, not the builder, accounts for the VAT.
- **Making Tax Digital (MTD):** HMRC's requirement for businesses to keep digital records and file through software.
- **Purchase order (PO):** the builder's order to a merchant or supplier.
- **Delivery note:** the paper that comes with a delivery listing what arrived.
- **Merchant:** a builders' merchant or supplier.
- **Credit note:** a document reducing what's owed, instead of a cash refund.
- **Listed building / conservation area:** properties or areas where extra planning rules apply.

### JobGuard words

- **Walk it:** talking through the job on site to capture it.
- **Switch live:** the moment a won job becomes a live job in JobGuard. The accepted quote is locked as the baseline.
- **Baseline:** the accepted quote, locked when the job goes live.
- **Log an extra:** the one-tap way to record an extra. Anything logged is always fee-free.
- **Customer approval:** the customer approving a quote or extra on their phone.
- **Decisions inbox:** the short list of things only the builder can decide.
- **Things to check:** problems JobGuard found, such as a supplier invoice that doesn't match the order or delivery.
- **Readiness warning:** an early warning that a site won't be ready on the day.
- **Proof:** dated photos and certificates filed against the right part of the job.
- **Final account:** built automatically from the baseline plus approved extras.
- **Complete final account:** the builder's button to confirm their final bill is complete. It saves the bill as it stands and starts the Final Check.
- **Final Check:** JobGuard's check, after the builder completes their final bill, for work that was done but isn't billed.
- **Catch:** an item the Final Check found.
- **Recovery case:** a case the builder opens when a customer won't pay money they owe.
- **Evidence pack:** a dated, checkable collection of everything about a job, for disputes.
- **Value receipt:** the monthly and per-job summary of what JobGuard carried through to the bill (fee-free) and what it found (where the 10% applies).
- **Jobs on the go:** the live jobs that count towards the monthly plan.
- **Extra job:** a job over the plan's number, charged £20 a month.
- **Small job:** a job worth under £2,000 when accepted; it doesn't take a place.
- **Pay-now link:** a link on an invoice that lets the customer pay by card or by bank in seconds.
