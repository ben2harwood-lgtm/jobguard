# JobGuard Sales Bible — Part 6: How to sell it

This is part 6 of the JobGuard Sales Bible: the practical playbook. Pitches, questions to ask, how to run a demo, how to handle objections, how to close, and the rules on what never to say.

## The pitches

### The 10-second version (merchant counter, van window)

"JobGuard makes sure you get paid for every extra and never overpay a merchant. Quoting's free, plans from £29 a month."

### The 30-second version

"Most builders lose money on every job without noticing: extras agreed on the doorstep and never billed, merchant invoices nobody checks, final bills sent weeks late. JobGuard fixes that. You talk through the job on site and it writes the quote. You log extras in seconds and the customer approves them on their phone. It checks your supplier invoices before you pay, and builds your final bill for you. Then it does a Final Check for anything you still missed. Quoting's free, it's from £29 a month, and your first job's free."

### The 2-minute version (sat down with an owner)

1. **The problem:** "How many jobs have you got on right now? … On those, how many extras has the customer asked for? … And how many of those are written down somewhere you'll see when you do the final bill?"
2. **The idea:** "JobGuard means you say the job once. Talk through it on site and it becomes the quote. Win it and it becomes the live job. Every extra goes in with one tap and the customer approves it on their phone. When you finish, your final bill is already built."
3. **The watchdog:** "While the job's running it checks your merchant invoices against what you ordered and what turned up, warns you when the site's not ready, and reminds you to take photos before anything gets covered up."
4. **The safety net:** "When you complete your final bill, JobGuard runs its own Final Check of your receipts, photos, notes and customer messages. If it finds work you did but didn't bill, it shows you the proof. You decide. If you bill it and the customer pays, we take 10%. Anything you logged yourself is always 100% yours."
5. **The price:** "Quoting's free. Your team's plan is £69 a month for up to seven jobs on the go, however many people you've got. Your first job's free, so put your next job on it and see."

## Discovery: questions to ask

Ask, listen, and let the builder tell you where they lose money. Don't rush to the demo.

**About their work**
- What kind of jobs do you do? How big is a typical job?
- How many jobs have you got on the go right now? How many people?
- Who does the quotes, the ordering, the invoices?

**About extras (the big one)**
- When a customer asks for something extra on site, what happens next? Where does it get written down?
- When did a customer last say "I thought that was included"?
- Do you think you've done extras you never charged for? Roughly how many last year?

**About materials**
- Do you have agreed prices with your merchants?
- When did you last check a merchant invoice line by line against the order and the delivery note?
- Ever paid for something that never turned up?

**About proof and disputes**
- Ever had to open something up because there was no photo?
- Ever had a customer dispute an extra or refuse to pay part of a bill? What happened?

**About getting paid**
- How long after finishing a job does the final bill go out?
- Is anyone holding back money from you right now? How much?

**About their tools**
- What do you use now for quotes and invoices? What do you like and not like about it?
- Is Making Tax Digital on your radar? What's your accountant said?

**The closing question**
- "If you could fix one of those tomorrow, which would it be?"

## The demo (10 minutes, on a phone)

Keep it short and about their work. Use a job like theirs.

1. **Walk it (2 minutes).** Talk through a small job into the phone: "Rear extension, three by four metres, strip foundations, new bifolds, move the radiator, make good the kitchen wall." Show the list JobGuard produces, with each line linked to what was said, and the gap it flags ("radiator moved, but no pipework line").
2. **Quote it (1 minute).** Prices from their rates, the professional quote, send. "Free. Always."
3. **Switch live and Log an extra (2 minutes).** "Customer asks for an outside tap." Tap Log an extra, say it, price it, send it for approval. Show what the customer sees on their phone. "That's now on your final bill. You'll never forget it, and they approved it in writing."
4. **Things to check (1 minute).** Show a supplier invoice matched against the order and the delivery: "Charged £1.80 a metre, you agreed £1.20. Caught before you paid. That's included in the plan."
5. **Proof (1 minute).** Show the photo reminder before plastering and the dated photo filed against the job.
6. **Final account and Final Check (2 minutes).** Show the final account built automatically. Complete it. Show the Final Check finding: the diary note "put two extra sockets in the utility", the photo, not on the bill. "You decide. Bill it and you keep 90%. Anything you'd logged was 100% yours."
7. **Close (1 minute).** "Your first job's free. What's the next job you're starting?"

**Demo rules:** let the builder hold the phone. Use their words and their jobs. Stop when they say "that'd have saved me on the …" and let them tell the story.

## Handling objections

Answer briefly and honestly, then ask a question back.

**"I've already got Tradify / Powered Now / Fergus."**
"They're good for booking and invoicing. Do they check your merchant invoices against the order and the delivery? Build your final bill from the approved extras automatically? Do a Final Check for anything you missed? That's what JobGuard's for. And it's one price for the whole team, not per person."

**"I don't forget extras."**
"Brilliant — then log them and they're 100% yours, and JobGuard costs you £29 or £69 a month for the checks and the paperwork. What about your lads — do they tell you every extra the customer asks them for on site?"

**"Another monthly subscription."**
"Fair. It's about a pound a day on Solo. One unbilled extra or one merchant overcharge a month and it's paid for itself. And your first job's free, so you'll know before you pay a penny."

**"10% of my money? No chance."**
"It's 10% of money you'd never have seen. You keep 90%. Anything you log yourself is always 100% yours. And we only take it once your customer's actually paid."

**"So you're hiding things from me until the end?"**
Be completely straight: "During the job JobGuard notices things, and unless it's urgent it keeps them for the Final Check after you've done your own bill. That's the deal: log it yourself and it's free; our check afterwards costs 10% of what you get paid for it. If anything's urgent — safety, big extra work that needs your customer's OK first, or an argument brewing — we tell you straight away and never charge on it. It's all in the terms from day one."

**"Why don't you just tell me as I go?"**
"Because then you'd be paying for us to do your job for you. This way you're in control: capture it yourself and it's free. The Final Check is a second opinion after you've done your own bill."

**"My customers won't use an app."**
"They don't need one. They get a link on their phone to approve the quote and extras. One tap. Most homeowners prefer it — they can see exactly what they're paying for."

**"I'm not techy."**
"If you can send a voice note on WhatsApp, you can use JobGuard. You talk; it writes."

**"What about signal on site?"**
"The app works with no signal. Photos, voice notes and extras save on your phone and upload when you're back in signal."

**"What happens to my data?"**
"It's yours. It's stored in the UK and EU, only your company can see it, and you can export it. We never sell it and never share your customers' details."

**"Does it handle the money?"**
"No. Your customers pay straight into your own account through a regulated payment provider. JobGuard never holds your money."

**"Will it chase my customers for me?"**
"It drafts the reminders and letters, but nothing goes out without your approval. It's your customer and your name — you're always in control."

**"Can it do my VAT / CIS / accounts?"**
"It puts the right VAT and CIS on your invoices and sends the figures to Xero or QuickBooks. Your accountant still does your accounts and gives you tax advice."

**"I'll think about it."**
"Of course. What would you need to see to be sure? … Tell you what: your next job's free. Put it on, and if it doesn't catch anything, you've lost nothing."

**"Can I get a discount?"**
"The price is the price — it's the same for everyone. What I can do is get your first job on free so you can see what it's worth to you."

## Closing

- **The natural close is the free first job.** "What's the next job you're starting? Let's put it on JobGuard today."
- **Set it up with them there and then:** add a user or two, quote their next job together, show them Log an extra.
- **Ask for the card at switch-live**, when they're committed to the job. Mention "skip for now" is there, but the card's needed before a second job goes live.
- **Book a check-in** for a week into the first live job and again at the Final Check.
- **Make sure they've heard the 10% rule** before they sign up. Confirm: "Just so it's clear…"

## After the sale

- **Week 1:** Check they've logged at least one extra and taken proof photos.
- **At the first final account:** be there (phone or in person) for the Final Check. Whatever it shows — even nothing — explain it.
- **Month 1:** Show them their value receipt: "Extras you logged and we carried through to your bill: £X, fee £0."
- **Ask for referrals** once they've had a good Final Check or caught a merchant overcharge: "Who else do you know who'd want this?"

## The golden rules

### Always
- **Explain the Final Check and the 10% before sign-up**, every time, in plain words.
- **Tell builders to use Log an extra for anything they want to bill** — it's always free.
- **Answer honestly if asked whether JobGuard spots things during the job.** Yes, it does, and it keeps them for the Final Check unless they're urgent.
- **Say the price plainly.** Ex VAT.
- **Be honest about fit.** If ServiceM8 suits them better, say so.

### Never
- **Never promise savings.** No "JobGuard will save you £5,000". Say "here's how it catches this" and let them work out the value.
- **Never say "you'd have forgotten this" or "you'd never have billed that".** We can't know.
- **Never call records "tamper-proof", "court-ready", "legally approved" or "bank-verified".** Say "a dated, checkable record of the whole job".
- **Never call JobGuard a debt collector or claims company**, and never suggest it will take anyone to court.
- **Never give legal or tax advice.** Point them to their solicitor or accountant.
- **Never say JobGuard holds or handles customers' money.**
- **Never promise a feature, extra or price that isn't on the current list.** Check with Ben.
- **Never offer a discount or special deal** that isn't on the price list.
- **Never run down other products.** Talk about what JobGuard does that they don't.
- **Never suggest JobGuard takes money from merchants, lenders or anyone else** to recommend them. It doesn't, and that's why builders can trust its invoice checks.
- **Never call the subscription "free".** Quoting is free; the first job is free; the plans are paid.
