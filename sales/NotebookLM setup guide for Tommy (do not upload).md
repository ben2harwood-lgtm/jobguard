# NotebookLM setup guide for Tommy

This guide is for setting up the notebook. **Don't upload this file into NotebookLM** — upload only the eight "Sales Bible" files.

## 1. Create the notebook (5 minutes)

1. Go to **notebooklm.google.com** and sign in with a Google account.
2. Click **New notebook** and name it **JobGuard Sales Bible**.
3. Click **Add source → Upload** and select all eight files from the `JobGuard Sales Bible` folder:
   - 01 What JobGuard is and how it works
   - 02 How JobGuard saves builders money
   - 03 The Final Check and the 10 percent fee
   - 04 Pricing and plans
   - 05 Who to sell to
   - 06 How to sell it
   - 07 The market and the competition
   - 08 FAQ and glossary

   If NotebookLM won't accept `.md` files, rename them to end in `.txt`, or open each one and use **Add source → Copied text** to paste it in.

**Don't upload the monetisation report or the pricing research.** They're Ben's internal working papers, with rejected ideas, internal economics and internal nicknames. If they're in the notebook, NotebookLM will mix them into Tommy's summaries and audio, and Tommy may repeat internal material to builders. Everything Tommy needs is in the bible.

## 2. Tell the notebook its job

In the chat panel, open the settings (the **Configure notebook** or chat settings icon), choose a **custom** style, and paste:

> You are a sales coach training Tommy, a new salesperson for JobGuard, a software product for UK builders. Use only the sources. Speak in plain, everyday English, like one builder talking to another. Keep answers short and practical. When Tommy asks how to answer a builder, give the exact words to say. Always follow the rules in "06 How to sell it": never promise savings, always explain the Final Check and the 10% fee honestly, never give legal or tax advice. If the sources don't cover something, say "Check with Ben" instead of guessing.

## 3. Make these learning materials

Use the **Studio** panel on the right. Each tool has a pencil or "Customise" option where you can paste instructions. Make them in this order. (Button names may differ slightly depending on your NotebookLM version.)

### Audio Overview 1 — "The big picture" (Deep Dive, default length)

> Explain JobGuard to a new salesperson who has never worked in software. Start with the seven ways builders lose money, then walk through a builder's job from site visit to getting paid. Spend real time on the Final Check and the 10% fee: exactly what is always free (anything logged with Log an extra or added before completing the final account) and what can earn 10%. Finish with the pricing. Use the £30,000 extension example. Plain English, no jargon.

### Audio Overview 2 — "The sceptical builder" (Debate or Deep Dive)

> One host is a sceptical UK builder with 5 staff who already uses Tradify and hates subscriptions. The other is a JobGuard salesperson. The builder raises every objection in "06 How to sell it" and "03 The Final Check and the 10 percent fee", especially "so you hide things from me until the end?", "10% of my money?" and "I don't forget extras". The salesperson answers honestly and simply, exactly as the sources say. Show how to handle each objection without overpromising.

### Audio Overview 3 — "Daily refresher" (Brief)

> A 5-minute refresher of the facts a JobGuard salesperson must never get wrong: the two plans and their prices, the 7-job line and the contractor deal, how jobs are counted, the free first job, what the 10% applies to and what it never applies to, and the "never say" list.

### Video Overview (if available)

> A simple explainer video for a new salesperson showing how a job flows through JobGuard: walk it, quote it, switch live, log an extra, supplier invoice check, proof photos, final account, Final Check, invoice and payment. Show clearly which parts are free and where the 10% fee can apply.

### Mind Map

Click **Mind Map**. Use it to see how everything connects: the product, the leaks, the pricing, the buyers, the objections.

### Reports

Create each of these under **Reports** (choose "Create your own" and paste the prompt where there's a custom option):

1. **Study Guide** (built-in): key terms, short-answer questions and essay questions.
2. **Briefing Doc** (built-in): the one-document summary.
3. **One-page cheat sheet (custom):**
   > Make a one-page cheat sheet a JobGuard salesperson can keep on their phone: the 10-second and 30-second pitches, the two plans and prices, the 7-job line and the contractor deal, the one-breath explanation of the Final Check and 10%, the top 8 objections with a one-line answer each, and the "never say" list.
4. **Objection cards (custom):**
   > List every objection a builder might raise about JobGuard found in the sources. For each: the objection in the builder's words, the answer in plain words to say out loud (under 40 words), and a question to ask back.
5. **Discovery script (custom):**
   > Turn the discovery questions into a conversation script for a first meeting with a small building firm owner and their partner who does the books, including how to move from questions to the demo.
6. **Buyer profiles (custom):**
   > Describe the three builder types (sole trader, small firm, bigger firm on the contractor deal) as realistic people: their day, their money leaks, what they want to hear, the plan that suits them, and the best opening line for each.

### Flashcards

> Make flashcards on the facts a salesperson must know by heart: plan prices and included jobs, extra-job prices, the monthly maximums, how jobs are counted, small jobs under £2,000, the free first job, exactly what earns 10% and what never does, the must-tell-straight-away situations, the seven money leaks, competitor prices, and the "never say" rules.

### Quiz

> Make a 25-question quiz testing whether a new JobGuard salesperson is ready to sell: pricing, how jobs are counted, the Final Check and 10% rules, which builders are and aren't a good fit, how to handle objections, and what they must never say. Include tricky scenario questions, such as "A builder logged an extra as a draft and never priced it; the Final Check finds it — is there a fee?" (Answer: no.)

Tommy should retake the quiz until scoring at least 23 out of 25.

### Infographic or slide deck (if available)

> One-page visual: "How JobGuard gets builders paid for everything", showing the job flow and, clearly marked, what is always free and where the 10% applies.

## 4. Practise in the chat (role-play)

Paste these into the notebook chat one at a time. Tommy types answers as if talking to the builder, then asks for feedback.

1. > Play a sole trader doing bathrooms who's never used software and thinks apps are a waste of money. I'm going to pitch you JobGuard. Push back realistically. After 6 exchanges, stop and grade my pitch out of 10 against the rules in "06 How to sell it", including anything I said that I shouldn't have.
2. > Play the partner of a small builder who does all the invoices and chasing. You're interested but worried about the 10% fee and about JobGuard "hiding things". Question me hard, then grade my answers for honesty and clarity.
3. > Play the owner of a 15-person firm running 15 jobs at once. Ask me which deal applies to you, what it costs and why. Then tell me which deal I should have recommended and whether my explanation was right.
4. > Play a builder who uses Tradify and is happy with it. Tell me why you don't need JobGuard. Then grade how I handled it: did I run down Tradify? Did I find the gap?
5. > Give me 10 quick-fire scenarios, such as "the customer pays half of a Final Check item" or "the merchant gives a credit note, not a refund". For each, I'll say what JobGuard charges. Mark each answer right or wrong using the sources.
6. > Ask me to explain the Final Check to you in under 30 seconds as if you're a builder at a merchant counter. Tell me what I got wrong or left out.

## 5. A five-day learning plan

| Day | What Tommy does | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Listen to Audio Overview 1. Read Parts 1 and 2. Look at the Mind Map. | 1 hour |
| 2 | Read Part 3 (the Final Check) twice. Listen to Audio Overview 2. Do role-play 2. | 1 hour |
| 3 | Read Parts 4 and 5. Flashcards. Do role-plays 3 and 5. | 1 hour |
| 4 | Read Parts 6 and 7. Learn the 30-second pitch by heart. Do role-plays 1, 4 and 6. | 1 hour |
| 5 | Take the Quiz until scoring 23/25 or more. Record the 2-minute pitch on the phone and play it back. Run a practice demo with Ben. | 1–2 hours |

After that: Audio Overview 3 on the way to appointments, and a role-play the night before a big meeting.

## 6. Ready to sell? The sign-off checklist

Tommy is ready when able to:

- [ ] Give the 10-second, 30-second and 2-minute pitches without notes
- [ ] Explain the Final Check and the 10% in one breath, including what's always free
- [ ] Answer "so you hide things from me?" honestly and calmly
- [ ] Quote both plans, their included jobs, the extra-job price, and when a firm moves to the contractor deal
- [ ] Explain how jobs are counted, including small jobs and the free first job
- [ ] Say who JobGuard is and isn't for
- [ ] Run the 10-minute demo on a phone
- [ ] Handle the top 10 objections
- [ ] List the "never say" rules
- [ ] Score 23/25 or more on the Quiz

## 7. Keeping it up to date

When Ben changes prices, plans or features, replace the affected Sales Bible file in the notebook (delete the old source, upload the new one) and regenerate the cheat sheet, flashcards and quiz. Old audio overviews will still have the old facts, so delete and remake them too.
