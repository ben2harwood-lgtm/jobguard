The shape of the problem

The build order isn't a preference — it's the value chain.

JobGuard's promise is a strict dependency chain: you can't watch a job that doesn't exist yet, and you can't bill a recovery before the machinery that proves the money actually landed. The plan simply follows that chain. Everything that is irreversible once field phones hold data or once money and evidence records exist gets built first.

  • Hold the job first. No capture without the Job + immutable scope_item_id spine and the offline substrate to hold it on a no-signal site.
  • Then structure it. No structured capture without the intake pipeline — speech-to-text → AI extraction → human confirm — sitting on the Integration Gateway.
  • Then price and switch it live. No quote without estimating over that spine; no £79 charge without the switch-live hook and the double-entry money ledger.
  • Then watch it. The watchdog can only check a job that already exists — so it follows capture and quoting, never precedes them.
  • Then, and only then, bill a recovery. A recovery fee needs 3-way match (the claim), evidence (the proof) and bank reconciliation (the cash landed) — so the fee meter is deliberately late.
  • Freeze the irreversibles on day one. The sync contract, the tamper-evident hash chains and the scope_item_id spine cannot be repainted later. They are Phase 0 whatever else slips.

Guiding cut for the whole plan: spend the risk budget forward on the two hardest, most differentiating paths — the voice-capture intake spine and the recovery-billing engine — and buy or abstract everything that isn't a moat (speech-to-text, the language model, invoice reading, payments, open banking, e-signature) behind one gateway from the first line of code.

Phase detail

Goal, workstreams and the exit that ends each phase.

A phase isn't done when the code is written — it's done when the exit criterion is demonstrably true. Two of those exits are the milestones the whole company is judged on.

P0
Irreversible foundations
Months 0–4 · built under and before P1
Goal

Stand up every decision that is expensive or impossible to reverse once phones are in the field, money records exist, or evidence has been captured — so everything after builds on bedrock, never sand.

Workstreams
Infra + region pinning — Terraform, ECS Fargate, Aurora Postgres, S3 Object-Lock, Temporal, Cognito, KMS, all in eu-west-2 Multi-tenant isolation — tenant_id on every row, Postgres RLS, and the adversarial cross-tenant test harness as a hard CI merge gate The spine — Job aggregate + immutable scope_item_id, append-only event log + transactional outbox Offline substrate + frozen sync contract — encrypted SQLite, PowerSync, the conflict/event-fold engine, the separate command outbox Evidence integrity — hash-on-capture, WORM storage, per-tenant evidence hash chain Audit chain + anchoring skeleton — per-tenant hash-chained AuditEvent, Merkle anchoring to a timestamping authority Money primitives — integer-pence GBP, one rounding rule, the append-only double-entry ledger, the concurrency ledger Integration Gateway — the anti-corruption layer so no business module ever calls a vendor SDK directly
Exit criteria
Two-tenant isolation suite gates every merge; an offline row syncs and folds deterministically through a simulated multi-crew conflict; a photo is hashed on device, re-hashed and WORM-locked server-side; an AuditEvent verifies against a recomputed chain and anchors to a test TSA; the ledger holds debits==credits under fuzz; the frozen sync contract is written down, test-enforced and signed off.
P1
MVP — the “one live job” loop
Months 2–9 · the centre of gravity
Goal

Prove the entire promise on a single live job, end to end, unscripted and offline-tolerant: walk it → quote it free → switch it live for £79 → protect it with the internal-signal watchdog → get paid. Nothing canonical until a human confirms; nothing sent, ordered or billed without an explicit yes.

Workstreams
Identity + auth — Cognito SMS-OTP, crew invites, offline capture-only sessions “Walk it” capture — one-thumb voice + inline photo, fully offline, hashed and queued Intake pipeline — STT adapter, numeric-safe post-correction, AI structured extraction, the flagged-question engine, a golden-set eval gate Review + confirm — the “nothing dropped” reconciliation screen; accept is the human-in-the-loop boundary Estimating + quoting — quote builder over the spine, seeded rate-book, UK VAT/DRC/CIS presentation, coverage-diff, PDF, Decision-gated send Win + switch-live — baseline freeze, won→live hand-off, the flat £79 charge, and the recovery-fee cap fixed for the job Variations + proof-of-cover — the hard gate that a stage can't complete without a proof photo; on-site written approval Watchdog + Decision Inbox — the four internal signals, suppression v1, the single Resolution API → Command Dispatcher path Final account — assembled from the frozen baseline + evidence pack, mark-paid
Exit — Milestone A
A real builder walks a job by voice + photos offline; it becomes a structured proposal with flagged questions; they confirm it with “nothing dropped” visible; build, price and send a free quote; mark it won (baseline freezes); switch live and are charged exactly £79 with no surprise; resolve a genuine watchdog decision; capture proof-of-cover and log an extra as a variation; the same one record becomes a final account with evidence attached and is marked paid. No re-typing anywhere. Every outbound a deliberate yes. It runs unscripted.
P2
Watchdog depth
Months 9–14
Goal

Make the watchdog earn its name: catch a real loss the builder didn't see, from outside data — a merchant invoice, a site-readiness signal — and surface it as a genuine, ranked, de-noised decision rather than noise.

Workstreams
Materials 3-way match (leak 3) — PO builder, pre-commit rate checks, invoice OCR, goods-received capture, the line-matching + 3-way match engines Site readiness (leak 4) — the dependency model, daily evaluation, sequencing + lead-time knowledge base, labour-gap roll-up, resequencing Inbox depth — suppression v2, AI decision-copy, urgency classifier, SMS + WhatsApp, escalation and routing, the signal-to-noise dashboard as a standing gate Crew multi-user — concurrent editing and conflict UX exercised for real Rate-book assist + observation capture — static pricing help now, with actuals-capture wired on so the moat can compound
Exit criteria
The materials engine surfaces a real overcharge / short delivery from a photographed invoice as an actioned Decision, below the tuned false-positive threshold; the readiness engine flags a genuinely not-ready tomorrow early enough to resequence, with labour-£-at-stake shown, without crying wolf; Finding→Decision conversion sits in the healthy band on real usage.
P3
Recovery + billing engine
Months 14–21 · highest complexity, do not compress
Goal

Turn a detected leak into money that provably lands back in the builder's account — and only then emit a billable event. “No proof, no fee,” the 1.5% cap fixed at switch-live, and the once-only plan-fee credit become structural impossibilities to violate, not checks someone can forget.

Workstreams
Recovery case engine — the case state machine, the billability classifier, the evidence-bundle assembler, the tamper-evident pack Regulator-safe pursuit — the AI message drafter with tone guardrails, the human-approval send flow, the Temporal recovery saga; the builder is always the sender Landed-money detection — open-banking feed + reconciliation matcher + the structural guard that a fee is impossible without landed evidence Outcome billing — the deterministic recovery-fee derivation (no AI in the money path), concurrent-live tiering + upgrade decisions, Stripe + GoCardless, VAT invoicing, dunning, the “see the proof” statement Full customer billing — interim applications + Construction Act notices, retention release on durable timers, full CIS/DRC, withheld-payment handoff Audit hardening — live TSA anchoring, DSAR + legal-hold, first money-surface pen test, the regulatory-controls module
Exit — Milestone B
A real supplier overcharge (or withheld customer payment) is detected, classified billable, assembled into a defensible pack, pursued via a builder-approved message, and the credit/refund actually lands and is machine-reconciled to hash-chained landed evidence. Exactly one recovery-fee row is written — 10p/£1, capped at the 1.5% fixed at switch-live, plan fee credited once — reproducible, disputable, VAT-correct, and impossible to have fired without proof. A prevented miss produces no fee. Legal has signed off the perimeter posture.
P4
Scale + integrations
Months 21–27
Goal

Make JobGuard sit cleanly beside the builder's existing accounting and supplier stack, and switch the rate-book learning loop on so the moat compounds from the first completed job.

Workstreams
Accounting — Codat aggregator + direct Xero then QuickBooks, with a VAT/DRC/CIS/retention tax-code mapping UI and human-confirmed deduped pushes Merchants — hardened document ingestion, punchout + PEPPOL + top-merchant portals where they exist, a second open-banking provider Rate-book learning on — the shrinkage drift estimator, recurring-extra detection, merchant-reliability scoring — suggestions only, human-accepted Sync + scale hardening — conflict resolution at scale, low-end-Android performance floor, backups/DR, Contractor-scale load
Exit criteria
A builder's final account posts correctly (VAT/CIS/DRC/retention) into Xero without re-keying and deduped; the rate-book produces an accepted suggestion that measurably tightens the next similar quote; the system holds performance and sync integrity at Contractor scale on the phones builders actually carry.
P5
Enterprise
Months 27–33+
Goal

Let a 40+-live-job contractor adopt JobGuard under their own security, identity and audit requirements.

Workstreams
Enterprise identity — SSO (SAML/OIDC), fine-grained roles + org hierarchies via a Cedar policy engine, on-call routing Isolation graduation — per-tenant KMS keys, schema-/DB-per-tenant, transparent behind the repository layer Trust exports — independently-verifiable signed audit trails, custom recovery caps/contracts, PO/net-30 invoicing, a public API Certifications — Cyber Essentials Plus → ISO 27001 / SOC 2, second pen test, white-glove onboarding
Exit criteria
An enterprise contractor runs 40+ concurrent live jobs under SSO and their own KMS, and exports a signed audit trail that verifies independently.

The non-negotiables

Ten invariants that are set in Phase 0 and never bent.

These are the decisions that are catastrophic or impossible to reverse once phones hold data and money records exist. They are enforced structurally — in the database, the type system and the physical separation of code — not by policy or discipline.

Tenant isolation is a DB invariant

Every row carries tenant_id; Postgres row-level security makes a cross-tenant read impossible even with a buggy handler. An adversarial two-tenant test suite is a hard CI merge gate before any business table ships.

RLS · default-deny · CI-gated

Human-in-the-loop is architectural

Watchdog checks are pure, side-effect-free functions that only emit Findings. The code that places an order, sends a message or bills is physically separate and reachable only by an authenticated user command resolving a Decision.

Nothing sent without a yes

The one job record spine

A single immutable scope_item_id carries from voice capture through quote, live job and invoice — so nothing is ever copied or re-typed, and evidence attaches once and appears everywhere.

Say it once

Two audit logs, one hash-chained

An append-only event log drives read models and the watchdog; a separate per-tenant hash-chained AuditEvent trail, Merkle-anchored to an external timestamping authority, gives dispute-grade tamper evidence for approvals, recoveries and every fee.

Legally defensible

The frozen sync contract

The offline ids, conflict rules and schema evolution are frozen day one, because changing them once devices hold data is a fleet migration. Three data classes: field last-writer-wins, append-only, and deterministic state-machine fold.

Freeze before the fleet

Money is integer pence, double-entry

Never floats. An internal append-only double-entry ledger is the source of truth for every fee; Stripe and GoCardless are collectors reconciled against it. UK reality — DRC, CIS, retention, interim applications — is modelled fully.

Pennies conserved under fuzz

Evidence is WORM + hashed at capture

Every photo is SHA-256 hashed on the device, stored write-once (S3 Object Lock), and its hash chained. Editing is a new object; GDPR erasure is a crypto-shred tombstone that preserves the chain and the fact-of-existence.

Write-once, provable

“No proof, no fee” is structural

A recovery-fee row can physically only be written from a case in state landed carrying hash-chained landed evidence. Prevention is a terminal state incapable of billing. The 1.5% cap is fixed at switch-live before any result can count.

Proof first, fee after

AI outputs are validated, cited, confident

Every model output is strict-JSON-schema validated with reject/repair, carries a citation to its source span or photo and a confidence score, and never becomes canonical without a human confirm. Golden-set evals gate CI so quality can't silently rot.

Cite or discard

UK residency + one gateway

Everything runs in eu-west-2, and every third-party capability (STT, LLM, OCR, payments, open banking, e-sign) sits behind an Integration Gateway with per-vendor residency tags — keeping every build-vs-buy choice reversible and auditable.

In-region · swappable
Gaps the review caught

The perimeter and the commercial reality — fold these in before committing.

The internal engineering spine is strong. The gaps are almost all where the product touches the outside world and its own business model: the builder's existing jobs, their customer, the legal basis of the fee, and the ways a free-tier-plus-outcome-fee model can be gamed. Several are Phase 0/1 blockers for the very Milestone A demo, not P4 polish.

High

No way to onboard a builder's in-flight jobs

A job can only be born via capture → quote → won → live. A real builder adopting the product already has 5–15 live jobs mid-flight — and the first thing every pilot user wants is to protect the work they're running now.

Fix Add an “adopt an in-flight job” intake in P1/P2: create a Job at any phase with a stated accepted value, an attested baseline and an imported provenance flag (weaker evidence lineage, modelled explicitly). Fix the cap at import.

High

The customer is invisible — no counterparty or approval portal

Quote acceptance, variation sign-off and the withheld-payment recovery all legally require the customer to act, yet there's no counterparty entity, portal auth, or consumer-vs-commercial classification — and that classification is what gates the whole regulator-safe recovery workflow.

Fix Define a Customer/Counterparty context + a magic-link approval portal in P1. Capture every customer “yes” as a hash-chained, portal-authenticated event. Drive is_consumer_facing from the modelled counterparty type.

High

The free-tier + outcome-fee model is trivially gameable

A builder can run capture, quoting and management free forever by never switching live; the 1.5% cap is set from a builder-controlled accepted value they're incentivised to under-report; and a builder who sees a recovery coming can settle it outside the app.

Fix Treat business-model integrity as a first-class subsystem: instrument won-but-never-live, run-elsewhere, and accepted-value divergence; consider tying the cap to quoted value or adding a fee floor. Decide deliberately, don't discover it in production.

High

The fee has a regulatory opinion but no contract

The plan covers the FCA/claims-management perimeter, but not the ToS, the definition of “landed,” the charge mandate, or UK unfair-terms exposure — and many sole-trader builders are treated as consumers. Enforceability rests on versioned ToS acceptance no subsystem currently captures.

Fix Add hash-chained, versioned ToS/mandate acceptance as a P0/P1 primitive (reuse the audit chain). Draft the fee terms in parallel with the perimeter opinion — you fix the cap on jobs in P1, before any legal basis for the fee exists.

High

Disaster recovery is three words in P4

For a product whose whole value is a tamper-evident vault + a money ledger, a data-loss event before P4 destroys the proposition. Restoring hash chains + WORM + saga state without breaking the chains is non-trivial, and single-region residency is in tension with a credible DR posture.

Fix Move RPO/RTO targets, point-in-time recovery, cross-AZ replication, saga-state recovery and tested restore drills into P0/P1. Explicitly resolve residency-vs-DR (multi-AZ in eu-west-2; a documented decision on any cross-region backup).

High

No test strategy for the money paths

Property tests are named for the ledger, but not for the combinatorially nasty parts: recovery-fee derivation across cap × plan-credit × partial-landing × multiple recoveries; VAT/DRC/CIS/retention correctness; reconciliation-matcher accuracy; end-to-end offline→sync→money.

Fix Elevate a “money-correctness golden suite” and an accountant-validated tax suite to named P0/P3 deliverables with coverage gates. Add contract tests for every gateway adapter against vendor sandboxes, and reconciliation-drift alerting as a P3 acceptance criterion.

Med

No analytics pipeline behind the two must-have dashboards

The plan names the decision-inbox signal-to-noise and recovery-funnel dashboards but nothing feeds them, and there's no activation/retention analytics to judge the Milestone A pilot. Reusing the domain event log for this couples them badly.

Fix Stand up a lightweight, residency-safe product-analytics pipeline in P1, separate from the domain event log — the pilot literally can't be judged without it.

Med

The plan is purely engineering — no pilot or support

Milestone A hinges on “a real builder on a real site,” but there's no design-partner recruitment, feedback loop or success metric beyond “it runs,” and human support first appears in P3. P1 pilot users on a high-UX-risk field product need support immediately.

Fix Add a design-partner/pilot workstream running P1→P3 with instrumented cohorts and explicit activation metrics. Staff human support at P1, not P3.

Med

Where the seed rate-book comes from is hand-waved

P1 assumes a “seeded starter rate-book,” but a credible UK materials + labour price book is a real data-acquisition and licensing problem (SPON's / NRM data is copyrighted). Without a decent seed, the very first quote is poor and the “closer to right” promise fails on day one.

Fix Name rate-book seed acquisition as a P0/P1 dependency with a copyright/licensing review — license a price book, build from merchant price feeds, or partner. It has lead time and legal exposure.

Med

No data export / portability / offboarding

GDPR portability, anti-lock-in, and what a cancelling builder gets back are missing; DSAR is P3 and accounting export P4, but there's no general “export my jobs, evidence and invoices,” which a cancelling P1 pilot user will demand.

Fix Add a self-service export workstream earlier than P4, and explicitly reconcile WORM compliance-mode retention against statutory retention and GDPR erasure-via-crypto-shred.

Med

Integrity isn't authenticity — and there's a liability question

Tamper-evidence proves a photo wasn't altered after capture; it doesn't prove it's a genuine record of this job/stage. A builder could stage evidence to fabricate a supplier claim or defend against a customer, and JobGuard assembles the pack.

Fix Add cheap capture-authenticity signals (geo + device attestation + capture-time correlation) and, more importantly, a documented liability posture reviewed with legal: JobGuard organizes builder-supplied evidence; it does not attest truth.

Med

SMS/WhatsApp provisioning lead times could break signup

Crew auth is SMS-OTP-first in P1 and WhatsApp arrives in P2. UK registered sender IDs and WhatsApp Business template approval have long lead times — if provisioning slips, signup itself breaks.

Fix Make the SMS provider + sender registration an early P0/P1 procurement item; start WhatsApp BSP onboarding at the beginning of P2, not when the feature is coded.

Five sequencing fixes to bank now: (1) PowerSync's replication typically reads the WAL via a role that bypasses RLS — spike whether tenant isolation actually holds on the sync path in P0, before declaring the isolation harness covers it. (2) Rate-book observation capture must be writing from the first completed job in P1/P2, even though learning suggestions land in P4 — otherwise P4 has nothing to learn from. (3) Shape the P1 authorization seam for the eventual fine-grained/Cedar model, or you rebuild permission checks everywhere and pay for it twice. (4) Start TrueLayer (open-banking) procurement in P2 — app review + 90-day reconsent have real lead time. (5) Serialize the once-only plan-fee credit per job (advisory lock / single writer / one workflow per job) so two concurrent recoveries can't each apply it.

Top build risks

What could sink it, and how each is de-risked.

High

Speech-to-text accuracy on accents, site noise and trade jargon

Measurement mishears are financially dangerous. De-risk a real-audio vendor bake-off before committing; construction keyword boosting; numeric isolation + aggressive flagging; the human confirm step as backstop; a golden-set gate in CI.

High

The offline conflict engine silently diverging money/state

Under real multi-crew editing. De-risk start it first; enforce the three-class policy end to end; property-based / multi-crew simulation testing before crews ever share a job offline; money, approvals and evidence are append-only and structurally can't conflict.

High

Over-billing on a false “landed” detection

Breaks “no proof, no fee” — existential and regulatory. De-risk landing requires a hash-chained evidence artifact + a confidence threshold + mandatory back-office review above a value threshold; an immutable, disputable derivation snapshot; prevention is a structural terminal state.

High

Recovery construed as regulated claims-management / debt collection

De-risk a legal perimeter opinion before P3 GA; the builder is always the sender; human approval on every contact; no dunning language (content-compliance classifier); an evidence-only posture; cadence caps and cooling-off for consumers.

High

Extraction hallucination or a silently dropped scope item

The exact leak we sell against. De-risk mandatory citations + confidence; the “nothing dropped” reconciliation; never auto-canonical; human confirm.

Med

A crying-wolf inbox — no-noise is existential

A noisy inbox kills the whole premise. De-risk suppression-first design; the signal-to-noise dashboard as a launch gate; per-user fatigue caps; hard money/safety floors personalization can never cross.

Med

RLS misconfiguration → cross-tenant leak

De-risk default-deny policies; an RLS-subject runtime role that is never owner/superuser; a migration lint; the adversarial isolation suite as a hard CI gate — and the separate PowerSync-path spike above.

Med

UK tax correctness (VAT/DRC/CIS/retention)

HMRC-adjacent and trust-destroying if wrong, on the builder's own invoices and in accounting pushes. De-risk in-house engines with accountant-validated golden suites; assist-not-assert UX; human-confirmed deduped pushes.

Med

Merchant API scarcity

The materials value prop rests on OCR + email ingestion. De-risk make the always-on document-ingestion tier excellent; treat any real API / punchout / PEPPOL as a bonus layer, won by BD not engineering.

Med

Line-matching & statement reconciliation accuracy

The two items most likely to overrun. De-risk confidence thresholds + tolerance bands + human-correctable matches; per-delivery matching first, consolidated statements as a hardened fast-follow.

Med

Long-lived sagas outliving their code

Recovery and retention timers run for months. De-risk Temporal versioning discipline with replay tests; durable scheduling that fires even on archived jobs.

Critical path & the two milestones

The company is judged on two demonstrations.

Milestone A — end of P1

One live job, unscripted

The full loop runs offline-tolerant on a single £79 live job: walk it, quote free, switch live, resolve a real watchdog decision, capture proof and a variation, invoice with evidence, mark paid — nothing re-typed. Proves desirability, the live-billing hook and the human-in-the-loop spine.

Milestone B — end of P3

A proven, capped, credited recovery

A real recovery is metered from a hash-chained landed event, capped at the fixed 1.5%, plan fee credited once, VAT-correct, reproducible and legally defensible. Proves the commercial engine and “no proof, no fee” as a structural guarantee.

The critical path to Milestone A:
offline substrate + conflict engine → the spine → Integration Gateway + STT/LLM adapters → capture → extraction + confirm → estimating + quote → win/freeze + switch-live + £79 hook → watchdog + Decision Inbox → final account + mark-paid.

Then extending to Milestone B:
3-way match + evidence bundle → recovery case + saga → landed detection + reconciliation → recovery-fee derivation + no-proof guard — gated by the legal perimeter opinion.

Two external tracks run in parallel off the engineering path and gate their phases: the real-UK-audio STT/extraction bake-off (before the intake pod commits) and the legal/regulatory perimeter opinion on recovery (must start no later than P2, gates P3 GA). Everything else — readiness, inbox depth, rate-book learning, integrations, enterprise — parallelizes off this spine.
Start these in week one

The decisions that gate everything else.

These are the load-bearing unknowns. Resolving them early is cheap; discovering them late is not. Most are not code — they're a spike, a procurement, or a lawyer.

Book the speech-to-text bake-off on real UK site audio

Accuracy is the single gating product risk. Record real accents, site noise and trade jargon and compare vendors before the intake pod commits.

Brief legal on the recovery perimeter and the fee contract together

Claims-management / debt-collection edge, plus the ToS, the definition of “landed,” the charge mandate, and sole-trader-as-consumer unfair-terms exposure. This gates P3 GA — start it now.

Spike PowerSync + RLS tenant isolation on the sync path

Replication commonly bypasses row-level security. Prove isolation holds on the mobile sync stream, or choose sync-rules/per-tenant buckets — before the substrate is frozen.

Settle how the seed rate-book is sourced

License a price book, build from merchant feeds, or partner — with a copyright review. First-quote quality depends on it, and it has lead time.

Decide the commercial-integrity stance on the cap and the fee

Is the cap set from a builder-editable accepted value, or from quoted value, or with a floor? Design the anti-leakage instrumentation before the model ships, not after.

Resolve residency vs disaster recovery, and the evidence key store

Single-region UK residency vs a credible RPO/RTO; and the per-evidence crypto-shred key store — now the single most sensitive asset — needs its own HSM, rotation and DR story.
