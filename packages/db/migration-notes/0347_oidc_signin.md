Its SAML branch retains the original raw assertion-ID lookup/insertion. All existing rows, indexes and replay constraints remain intact. All other statements, routine ownership/grants, SAML validation, SCIM, emergency and permission paths remain unchanged. The immutable connection fixes protocol/issuer for both subject and replay identity.

New identity/control-plane objects `identity.oidc_request` and append-only `identity.oidc_assertion_use` are tenant/connection-qualified, migration-owned, forced RLS, with no direct identity/business/infrastructure grants. Only migration-owned security-definer routines access its PKCE, nonce, browser binding and explicit-link credentials. Routine search paths are `pg_catalog`. `oidc_create`, `oidc_save`, `oidc_claim`, `oidc_fail`, `oidc_complete` are executable only by the existing trusted identity validator role and owner; `oidc_link` is owner-only. This is the existing validator trust boundary, not protection against a compromised identity credential or migration owner. No new credential or role.

Lock order: resolve connection/tenant; tenant advisory lock (54); linking session/current membership or durable request; existing completion's email lock and identity/membership writes; audit head last. Exchange occurs after claim commit and client release. Completion and its audit share a transaction. A completion rollback leaves the earlier claim consumed. A crash leaves `claimed`; this is a terminal, potentially unknown exchange outcome and never authorizes retry. Recorded `outcome_unknown` likewise requires a new request.

Completion creates a one-use bridge request for the existing `sso_complete` authority. Replay identity includes OIDC, immutable connection and unpredictable state, without requiring `jti`. Subject keys retain SCIM's SHA-256 subject contract: the composite tenant/connection key supplies the immutable protocol and issuer qualification. No SAML connection can change protocol. Verified no-group revocation commits before the public refusal. Session expiry is calculated from the database clock inside completion.
